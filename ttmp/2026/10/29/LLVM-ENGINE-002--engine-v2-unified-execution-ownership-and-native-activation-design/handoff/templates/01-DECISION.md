# Decision: <question>

## Status and scope

Proposed / accepted for revised design / deferred / rejected. State the admitted language and runtime profile.

## Current evidence

Name source revision, files/symbols, paper sections actually read, and historical results. Separate observation from inference.

## Alternatives

Compare at least two plausible approaches on the same examples. State dependencies, semantic obligations, API size, debugging needs, and removal opportunities.

## Worked example and counterexample

Show input, representation, state transitions, observation, and an invalid alternative. Label pseudocode and derived traces.

## Decision and justification

State what changes in guides 01/02. Explain why the chosen design is simpler or more effective under explicit constraints.

## What could overturn this decision

Give a falsifiable objection, missing evidence, and bounded research to resolve it.

## Proposed future implementation gate

Describe acceptance criteria only. Implementation is outside the seminar assignment.
